The mandate starts here

When to bring me in

  • Portfolio performance is below plan, and the causes are unclear.
  • Ownership needs additional asset-management or operating leadership.
  • Property managers need clearer standards and accountability.
  • Reporting takes too much work and still leaves important questions unanswered.
  • A restructuring, acquisition integration or capital program needs experienced execution.
  • A systems, data or AI initiative needs someone who understands how the business operates.

Four areas of focus

What I take on

01 / Performance

Portfolio and asset performance

Budgeting, forecasting, revenue opportunities, expense control, arrears, asset plans and owner reporting. Turn operating information into priorities that improve net operating income and portfolio performance.

02 / Operations

Property-management operations

Operating standards, third-party manager oversight, building audits, service-request performance, contract management and inspection tracking. Establish clear expectations, useful measures and accountability.

03 / Delivery

Capital, procurement and risk

Capital planning, project delivery, RFPs, contractor oversight, cost control, quality assurance and closeout. Coordinate insurance claims, warranties and operational risk management.

04 / Systems

Systems, data and AI

Assess workflows, improve Yardi reporting and data quality, and implement practical automation. Connect operational needs with systems that teams can use and maintain.

How I work

My approach is forward deployed: I work inside your operating environment, alongside your team, with a defined mandate and measures of success.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Review the portfolio, people, workflows and available data. Establish what is happening and why.

  2. 02

    Prioritize

    Agree on the most valuable improvements, responsibilities and timetable.

  3. 03

    Implement

    Work with leadership and operating teams to put the changes into practice.

  4. 04

    Transfer

    Leave functioning systems, clear accountability and the knowledge needed to sustain the improvements.
