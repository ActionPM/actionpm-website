A short story about data

You have a lot of data.
You want to use it.

Sounds simple. It’s often not.

What people really want

Most companies say“We want better reporting.”

But what they really mean

“We want quicker answers to important questions.”

  • Not another dashboard.
  • Not another analyst.
  • Not another spreadsheet.

The obvious idea

Could we just take all the data… drop it into one big data pot… and ask a model to analyze it?

Yes.

And honestly, it might be pretty good.
Sometimes even very good.

But “pretty good” is not enough for real operations.

The red-leaf problem

Imagine a tree.

Some leaves are red. Some leaves are green. You ask one simple question.

  1. 01 / You ask

    “How many red leaves are there?”

    The answer comes back instantly. Good number. You move on.

    1,459

  2. 02 / Five minutes later

    “How many red leaves are there?”

    The exact same question. A different answer.

    1,104

  3. 03 / One more time

    “Okay, one more time.”

    Three answers to one question. Which one do you trust?

    1,543

An illustration of inconsistent answers, not a count of the leaves in this drawing.

Why did the answer change?

Maybe time changed it — leaves fell, some turned, some were counted differently. But there is a deeper issue.

What exactly counts as a red leaf?

And what counts as a leaf?

The real data problem

AI can analyze data. But first the system needs to know what the data means.

Otherwise the answer may sound confident while still being wrong.

That means agreeing on

  • Definitions 01
  • Categories 02

Clean data needs structure

Clean data does not appear by accident.
It comes from structure.

  • Taxonomy
  • Validation
  • Normalization
  • Checks & balances
  • Source tracking
  • Repeatable workflows

You cannot extract reliable answers from a black hole. You have to understand the whole process.

The adoption problem

The system has to earn trust.

A lot of people in property management do not love new technology. And many more do not trust AI. Especially when the real job is surviving the day.

What they worry about

  • It will be wrong.
  • It will be complicated.
  • It will replace people.
  • They already have enough to deal with.

The answer is not to make AI louder. The answer is to make the system…

  • Easy
  • Useful
  • Consistent
  • Transparent
  • Connected
  • Verifiable

People need to see why the answer is the answer.

The bigger idea

Once the structure is right, everything else gets easier.

But the foundation has to come first.
